Leave policy

Leave balances are configured by your HR administrator. Approved paid leaves are deducted from the relevant annual allowance.

Requests remain pending until your administrator approves or rejects them.

Annual leave entitlement is 24 days per year. Leave is counted in working days only; Saturdays and Sundays are weekly holidays and are not deducted from your balance.
